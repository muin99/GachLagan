01 / FAMILIAR BY DESIGN

Your words, in your language.

English and Bangla with Avro phonetic. A visible draft, familiar keys, and one deliberate Encrypt button.

02 / CLEAR ABOUT PRIVACY

Codes for fun. Keys for privacy.

Binary, hex, octal, Base64url, and Morse are labeled as public encodings. Private mode uses authenticated AES-256-GCM.

03 / ENTIRELY ON YOUR DEVICE

No middleman. No key recovery.

Share your key in person. Keep it in protected device storage, or use it for one session. Losing the key means losing access.
